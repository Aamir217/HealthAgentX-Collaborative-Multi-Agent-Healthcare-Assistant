# Common Laboratory Reference Ranges

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- Hemoglobin: 12.0-17.5 g/dL (varies by sex)
- Hematocrit: 36-52%
- White Blood Cell (WBC) count: 4.0-11.0 x10^9/L
- Platelets: 150-450 x10^9/L

Low hemoglobin (anemia) can present with fatigue, pallor, and shortness of
breath. Elevated WBC (leukocytosis) often indicates infection or
inflammation; low WBC (leukopenia) can indicate bone marrow suppression or
certain infections.

## Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP)
- Fasting glucose: 70-100 mg/dL
- Creatinine: 0.6-1.3 mg/dL (renal function marker)
- Sodium: 135-145 mmol/L
- Potassium: 3.5-5.1 mmol/L

Elevated creatinine suggests reduced kidney function and should prompt
caution with renally-cleared or nephrotoxic medications (e.g. metformin,
NSAIDs, contrast dye). Hyperkalemia (high potassium) can cause dangerous
cardiac arrhythmias.

## Lipid Panel
- Total Cholesterol: under 200 mg/dL desirable
- LDL ("bad" cholesterol): under 100 mg/dL optimal
- HDL ("good" cholesterol): above 40 mg/dL desirable
- Triglycerides: under 150 mg/dL normal

## Diabetes Markers
- HbA1c: 4.0-5.6% normal, 5.7-6.4% prediabetes, >=6.5% diabetes
- Fasting glucose: 70-100 mg/dL normal, 100-125 mg/dL prediabetes, >=126 mg/dL diabetes

## Thyroid
- TSH: 0.4-4.0 mIU/L. Elevated TSH suggests hypothyroidism; suppressed TSH
  suggests hyperthyroidism.
